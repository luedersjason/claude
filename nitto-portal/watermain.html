A2SWPPP inspections: Pinnacle prices it once, everyone carries the same numberOpen
Where
  • Plans 11/3/26 · Sheet C-6, SWPPP s.5.0 Inspection; General Information
Who edits
Pinnacle (proposal + plan sheet C-6); Owner (bid form)
Current text

"INSPECTIONS SHALL BE UNDERTAKEN BY QUALIFIED PERSONNEL PROVIDED BY THE CONTRACTOR ... A STORMWATER POLLUTION PREVENTION PLAN INSPECTION REPORT SHALL BE COMPLETED AND ADDED TO THE SWPPP. RAINFALL SHALL BE RECORDED ON THE SWPPP RAINFALL LOG."

"KEEPING PLANS CURRENT: THE CONTRACTOR SHALL AMEND THE PLAN WHENEVER THERE IS A CHANGE ..."

"CONTRACTOR SHALL SUBMIT A COMPLETED NOTICE OF TERMINATION TO OWNER FOR EXECUTION PRIOR TO THEIR FINAL PAY APPLICATION REQUEST."

Proposed

Pinnacle gives a fixed-fee proposal for SWPPP inspections (every 7 days and within 24 hours of a 1/2" rain), inspection reports, rainfall log, SWPPP amendments, and the Notice of Termination.

Recommended: Zilber contracts Pinnacle directly (add it to Pinnacle's CM estimate under condition 6d). Bid form note: "SWPPP INSPECTION AND REPORTING BY OWNER'S ENGINEER UNDER SEPARATE CONTRACT. CONTRACTOR SHALL MAINTAIN AND REPAIR ALL EROSION CONTROL MEASURES WITHIN 24 HOURS OF NOTICE."

Alternative: carry it in the bid as a fixed allowance line, e.g. "SWPPP inspections by Pinnacle Engineering per proposal dated __/__/__: $______ (all bidders carry this amount)". Contractor pays Pinnacle.

Sheet C-6 s.5.0: "INSPECTIONS SHALL BE UNDERTAKEN BY QUALIFIED PERSONNEL PROVIDED BY THE OWNER'S ENGINEER." Amend-the-plan and Notice of Termination move to the Engineer.

Why
Pinnacle gets the work and every bidder carries the same SWPPP cost. Contracting Pinnacle directly avoids a contractor markup on it and keeps the inspector working for you, not the contractor. Either way the Village counts it: condition 7 sizes the security on public improvements plus inspections and construction services.
