Issues to resolve

▶
Contract completion date is in the past High
Owner: Pinnacle
Standard Terms s.5 sets substantial completion at July 31, 2026, and liquidated damages ($500/day, s.5a) key off it. Set a date that leaves time for Village inspection and acceptance before Nov 15, 2027.
▶
Scope on the plans but not on the bid form High
Owner: Pinnacle
Plans call for tracer wire with access boxes behind hydrants, insulation where directed, street sweeping and wetland protection. The form also has no line for pressure testing, disinfection or traffic control at STH 31 / 108th. Either add lines or state they are incidental to items 1-19, so the Village's 120% is computed from complete prices. Not checked against Village standard specs.
▶
No bid instructions in the manual High
Owner: Pinnacle
No bid due date, delivery, bid validity period, or list of what comes with the bid. Ask bidders for a sample COI naming the Village, a schedule and a subcontractor list: these are what the Village reviews under condition 6c.
▶
Bid form numbering Med
Owner: Pinnacle
Item 17 appears twice and 18 is missing, but the subtotal reads items 1-19. The Village itemizes the security from this form.
▶
Permits clause puts every permit on the contractor Med
Owner: Pinnacle
Terms s.7 says the contractor obtains all permits. Pinnacle is filing the WDNR extension, and the WPDES NOI is the owner's. Name the contractor's permits: Village ROW, erosion control (letter 10c/d) and WisDOT if applicable.
▶
DA Exhibit D is the 8/11 plan set Med
Owner: Jason Lueders
The contract cites the Nov 3, 2026 plans. Ask Peggy to swap the bid set into Exhibit D when the DA is finalized.
▶
Pinnacle CM estimate not seen Med
Owner: Andy Shoaf
Requested from Andy 11/4. Condition 6d requires it before the Village can compute the security. It is separate from the construction contract.
▶
Owner entity spelling on the CSM Low
Owner: Pinnacle
The contract correctly says TI Investors of Lakeview Nitto Parcel LLC. The CSM dedication draft says "Lakeveiw". Condition 6c requires the contract name to match title.
▶
Two different Zilber entities Low
Owner: Jason Lueders
The DA party is TI Investors of Pleasant Prairie Holding Company LLC; the contract owner is TI Investors of Lakeview Nitto Parcel LLC (titled owner). Confirm with the Village that the titled entity is what they want on the contract.
▶
Manual cleanup Low
Owner: Pinnacle
Proposal form names "Pinnacle Enginering, Attn: Anthony Zanon" while the cover lists Andy Shoaf. Pay apps every 60 days (s.10a) but based on month-end work (s.10b). s.24 "proper action by its governing body" is municipal wording that doesn't fit an LLC owner.
