Approval path, from plan approval to acceptance

The Village sizes the financial security at 120% of the public improvements, inspections and construction-related services, from the signed contract and Pinnacle's construction management estimate (DA Article VI, Ordinance s.395-17; conditions 6 and 7). The DA, Memorandum of DA and Stormwater Maintenance Agreement are signed at closing and recorded before any permits for the watermain (conditions 8 and 9). The Village schedules the preconstruction meeting once all documents and permits are in (condition 11). Dates for bidding, contract, closing and construction are not set yet.
