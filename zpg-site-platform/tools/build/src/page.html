Approval path, from water main bid to permits

The DA must be executed and the water main financial security provided, and the CSM and Stormwater Maintenance Agreement recorded, before the Village issues any permits for the project (S&O condition 1). The Village sizes the security at 120% of the public improvements, inspections and construction-related services, from the signed contract (DA conditions 6 and 7). The Village schedules the preconstruction meeting once all S&O condition 5 submittals are in. The building permit must be issued within 6 months of the 8/17/26 approval. Bid, contract, closing and construction dates are not set.
