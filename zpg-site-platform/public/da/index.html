Approval path, from approved CSM to recorded documents

The Village schedules the closing once the revised CSM, public improvement plans and contracts/COI are approved (cond 8). At closing the DA with Tax Agreements C1-C3, the Memorandum of DA and the SWMA are signed and the financial security and cash payments delivered. The Village then releases the documents for recording in this order: Memorandum, Tax Agreements, CSM, SWMA; recorded copies within 72 hours (cond 9). No permits issue for the water main or the site before that.
