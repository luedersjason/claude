Shared team link

One private link for the whole team (Pinnacle, the Village). Anyone who opens it can see the site and save review decisions as "Project team". Making a new one turns the old one off.

Invite someone

A personal link works once, on one device. It does not expire. If it is forwarded, the second person gets "already used". Each person can stay signed in on two devices. Use "New link" to reissue.
